**Onye ozi ezoro ezo nke na-adịghị achọ nọmba ekwentị ma ọ bụ ndebanye aha.**
Naanị onye zitere na onye natara nwere ike ịgụ ihe dị n'ime mkparịta ụka ahụ. Ọbụna sava na-ebu ya n'etiti enweghị ike ịgụ ya.
Atụmatụ niile bụ n'efu, ọ dịghịkwa mgbasa ozi.

Nke a abụghị ibe weebụ; ọ bụ ngwanrọ a na-etinye ma na-eji (Windows / Mac / Linux / Android / iPhone / ihe nchọgharị).

## Iji jiri ya, ị na-etinye ya na sava nke gị

Breeze enweghị "sava ụlọ ọrụ". **Iji jiri ya, gị onwe gị ga-enye ebe nchekwa (sava) ma tinye Breeze n'ebe ahụ. Nke a bụ ụzọ eji ya n'ụzọ gọọmentị.**

- Ntọala ọkọlọtọ na-eji Cloudflare, ya mere **a chọrọ akaụntụ Cloudflare**. Usoro ndị a dị n'ime [akwụkwọ nduzi nwụnye](SELF_HOSTING-ig.md) (ị nwere ike ịhọrọ asụsụ ndị ọzọ site na mbido akwụkwọ nduzi ahụ).
- Were ngwa ahụ (Windows / Mac / Linux / Android / iPhone) site na [ibe nbudata](https://github.com/shizukutanaka/Breeze/releases/latest), jikọọ ya na sava nke gị, ma jiri ya.
- **Banyere ihe atụ ngosi (ọkwa eziokwu)**: Anyị etinyela ihe atụ ngosi (https://breeze-469.pages.dev/) maka nnwale ọrụ. Ihe dị n'ime ya bụ otu ngwanrọ ahụ, mana maka ojiji n'ezie, biko jiri sava nke gị. Ihe atụ ahụ nwere ike ịgbanwe ma ọ bụ kwụsị na-enweghị ọkwa mbụ. Ihe ị tinyere na sava nke gị agaghị emetụta site na nkwụsị ihe atụ ahụ.

Nkọwa ojiji zuru oke dị n'ime [akwụkwọ ntuziaka](MANUAL-ig.md) (ị nwere ike ịhọrọ asụsụ ndị ọzọ site na mbido akwụkwọ ntuziaka ahụ).

## Otu esi eji ya (usoro 3)

1. Mepee Breeze ị tinyere na sava nke gị (ma ọ bụ adreesị onye tinyere ya gwara gị), ma pịa "Malite na ID enweghị usoro" (ị nwere ike itinye aha ma ọ bụrụ na ịchọrọ). Naanị ya, nkwadebe agwụla (enweghị ndebanye aha, enweghị nọmba ekwentị). N'oge a, a na-emepụta "igodo" n'ime ngwaọrụ gị, ọ bụkwa njirimara gị.
2. Nye onye ị chọrọ ịgwa okwu ID gị ma ọ bụ koodu QR gị.
3. Ọ bụrụ na onye nke ọzọ emepela ngwa ahụ, unu ga-ejikọ ozugbo ma kwurịta okwu. Ọ bụrụ na o meghereghị ya, ozi ahụ na-anọ n'ime "igbe nchekwa" n'ezoro ezo, ọ ga-erukwa ya mgbe o meghere ya. Sava na-ebu igbe nchekwa ahụ enweghị ike ịgụ ihe dị n'ime ya. Akwụkwọ ozi ndị na-erughị na-ehichapụ onwe ha mgbe ụbọchị 7 gachara na kacha.

## Nkwa (ihe anyị na-agaghị eme)

- **Anyị agaghị egosi mgbasa ozi.** N'ime ngwa ahụ, ma ọ bụ ebe ọ bụla.
- **Anyị agaghị eso gị.** Enweghị nyocha ojiji, enweghị usoro mgbasa ozi, enweghị ire data, ma ọlị.
- **Onye mepụtara anaghị ejide data ndị ọrụ.** N'akụkụ sava, enweghị ọbụna ndepụta akaụntụ. Njirimara gị bụ naanị igodo dị n'ime ngwaọrụ gị.
- **Mkparịta ụka na-ezoro ezo n'etiti ngwaọrụ.** Ihe sava na-ejizi bụ naanị envelopu a na-enweghị ike ịgụ.

Anyị ga-edekwa oke ya n'eziokwu. Iji ziga, sava na-echekwa ozi ruo nwa oge (envelopu ndị na-erughị ruo ụbọchị 7, akara njikọ nkeji 5, ndekọ adreesị na ngwaọrụ ụbọchị 90, ozi otu na ndebanye aha ọkwa ụbọchị 30. Ha niile na-ehichapụ onwe ha, enweghịkwa ike ịgụ ihe dị n'ime ha). Nchekwa nkwado na igwe ojii gbanyụrụ na mbido. Ọ bụrụ na ị tinye ya na sava nke gị, ị nwere ike itinye nchekwa ahụ n'okpuru nchịkwa nke gị.

## Ihe ọ nwere ike ime

- Mkparịta ụka otu-na-otu, na mkparịta ụka otu (ruo mmadụ 1,000)
- Oku olu na oku vidiyo (oku otu ruo mmadụ 8)
- Akụkọ oku (a na-edebe naanị na ngwaọrụ a: naanị onye nke ọzọ, ntụziaka, oge na ogologo oge oku — ọ dịghị ihe ọ bụla n'ihe e kwuru. A naghị eziga ya na sava ma ọ bụ onye nke ọzọ)
- Iziga faịlụ (ruo 50MB na mkparịta ụka otu-na-otu. Enwere ike iziga na otu kwa, mana oke nha pere mpe)
- Iji ọtụtụ ngwaọrụ na ọtụtụ akaụntụ (dịka maka ọrụ na nke onwe. Ọnụọgụ ngwaọrụ na akaụntụ bụ n'efu na-enweghị oke)
- Nkwado na-akpaghị aka (mgbe ihe gbanwere, a na-echekwa nkwado ezoro ezo na-akpaghị aka na sava nke gị. Na ngwaọrụ ọhụrụ, ozugbo i weghachitere njirimara gị, a na-achọta nkwado na-akpaghị aka ma nye gị ya. Ntọala na-eduzikwa gị ịkwaga na ngwaọrụ ọhụrụ)
- Aha ị họọrọ maka kọntaktị ọ bụla (ị nwere ike ịdegharị aha kọntaktị ọ bụla, nke echekwara naanị na ngwaọrụ a. Ị nwekwara ike ịhọrọ ma a ga-egosi aha kọntaktị ọ bụla tọrọ maka onwe ya)
- Asụsụ 51 (Bekee na faịlụ asụsụ 50), a na-ahọrọ ya na-akpaghị aka ka o kwekọọ n'asụsụ ngwaọrụ gị
- Profaịlụ nwere foto, stika, ikesa ọnọdụ
- Ọrụ iwu dịka `/help` (ụdị 71)
- Ụdị ike dabara na batrị gị (na-akpaghị aka / nkịtị / ichekwa ike / mberede). Ka batrị na-ebelata, a na-agbatị oge nlele nnyefe ka batrị na-adị ogologo. Nchebe maka ọdachi na oge ndị ọzọ ị na-enweghị ike ịchaji
- Taabụ ndị dị n'okpuru na ekwentị (Mkparịta ụka / Oku / Kọntaktị / Kaadị m / Ntọala. Taabụ ndị a ga-egosi enwere ike ịhọrọ na ntọala)
- Ihuenyo ndị dị mfe ịgụ (nlebara anya na ọdịiche agba, nha ederede, na iji naanị kiiboodu rụọ ọrụ)

## Ọdịiche na ndị ozi ndị ọzọ

| | Breeze | Ọtụtụ ndị ozi |
|---|--------|--------|
| Ndebanye aha | Achọghị (njirimara bụ igodo n'ime ngwaọrụ) | A chọrọ nọmba ekwentị |
| Sava | Enweghị ike ịgụ ihe dị n'ime | Ntọala ya bụ ịtụkwasị ụlọ ọrụ na-arụ ya obi |
| Itinye na sava nke gị | Ọ bụ ihe achọrọ (ihe a na-etinye ma na-eji) | Agaghị ekwe omume |
| Imeghe ihe dị n'ime | Ihe niile meghere (akwụkwọ ikike MIT) | Ọtụtụ emechiri emechi |
| Ebe data dị | N'ime ngwaọrụ gị | Na sava ụlọ ọrụ na-arụ ya |

Ntụnyere zuru oke na Signal na LINE dị n'ime [ibe ntụnyere (Bekee)](../COMPARISON.md). Nkwupụta ọ bụla nke Breeze nwere "ụzọ iji kwado ya", edekwara isi ihe Breeze dara (ọnụọgụ ndị ọrụ, enweghị nyocha mpụga, wdg.) na-ezoghị ya.

## Ego itinye na sava nke gị

Breeze na-arụ ọrụ n'ime oke n'efu Cloudflare. Ọ dịghị kaadị banki achọrọ.

| Ihe | Oke n'efu | Ọ bụrụ na a kwụrụ ụgwọ |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Ebe nchekwa | Cloudflare Pages (n'efu) | — |
| Nhazi | Ruo ugboro 100,000 kwa ụbọchị | Ruo ugboro nde 10 kwa ọnwa |
| Nchekwa | Kwa ụbọchị: ịgụ ugboro 100,000, ide ugboro 1,000 | Kwa ọnwa: ịgụ ugboro nde 10, ide ugboro nde 1 |
| Nnyefe oku | N'efu ruo 20GB | $0.05 kwa GB |
| Ngalaba aha nke gị | Achọghị (a na-enye adreesị n'efu) | $10 kwa afọ |
| **Ngụkọta** | **$0 kwa ọnwa** (ruo ihe dịka mmadụ 20–40 na ojiji dị mfe) | **Site na $5 kwa ọnwa** (ihe dịka mmadụ 100 na ojiji dị mfe. Na nha mmadụ 1,000, $34–$222 kwa ọnwa dabere na ojiji) |

Otu ego si eto na nnukwu nha, na "ihe kpatara otu onye ọrụ anaghị ebu ego niile", dị n'ime [echiche ego na nkwụsi ike (Bekee)](../SUSTAINABILITY.md), ngụkọ ọ dabere na ya dịkwa n'ime [ụdị ego (Bekee)](../COST_MODEL.md).

Nwụnye na-amalite na otu iwu a (nkọwa n'ime [akwụkwọ nduzi nwụnye](SELF_HOSTING-ig.md)).

```bash
wrangler pages deploy . --project-name=breeze
```

## Nkọwa teknụzụ (maka ndị mmepe)

### Ụzọ nzoro

| Ọrụ | Ụzọ |
|-------|-----------|
| Mgbanwe igodo | X25519 (nkwado: P-256) |
| Mbinye aka | Ed25519 (nkwado: HMAC-SHA256) |
| Nzoro | AES-256-GCM |
| Mmepụta igodo | HKDF-SHA256 |
| Imelite igodo mkparịta ụka | Double Ratchet (otu ụzọ ahụ na Signal) |
| Igodo otu | Sender Key |
| Izo onye zitere n'aka onye nnyefe | Sealed Sender |

Akwụkwọ nkọwapụta bụ isi dị n'ime [SPEC.md](../../SPEC.md), ebe a ga-akọ adịghị ike na amụma dịkwa n'ime [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md).

### Nnwale na nlele ịdị mma

```bash
npm ci            # tinye ngwaọrụ mmepe
npm test          # mee nnwale
./validate.sh     # nlele ịdị mma (ngalaba 7: usoro ahịrịokwu, nchekwa, asụsụ, nrube isi ụzọ, wdg.)
./build.sh zip    # mepụta ZIP nkesa
```

## English

Breeze is an end-to-end encrypted messenger with no phone number, no registration, and no ads — every feature is free. There is no company server: to use Breeze, you host it on your own server and connect the apps to it. Your identity is a key pair on your device; relays handle only sealed envelopes they cannot read. Full text in English: [docs/i18n/README-en.md](README-en.md). The full, sourced comparison against Signal and LINE — including where Breeze loses — is in [docs/COMPARISON.md](../COMPARISON.md).
